# warm-local-service

Friendly and practical: what, where, when and how to reach us come first; warm photos, hours, a map and one obvious action. A starting grammar, not a template: bend it when the requirement or a reference asks for it, and write the deviation in RUN.md "Design decisions".

## Layout grammar

Answers first. The hero says what the business is, where and how to act, and the first screen after it shows the practical facts: address, hours, phone, how to book or order. Sections are plain and familiar (services, the team or the place, how it works, questions, contact) in a steady two-column rhythm with photos of the real place and people. The same action appears in the header, mid-page and at the end. A map or a clear address block and an hours card are standard. Tone is friendly and direct.

## Header and footer

Header: name, three or four links, the main action as a visible button and the phone number. Footer: columns with address, hours, links and credits; small, tidy and complete.

## Typography

A warm humanist sans or a soft serif for headings, a clear sans for text; medium scale and sentence case; friendly rather than dramatic. Body size is generous; key facts (hours, phone) are set larger and plain.

## Imagery

Honest photographs of the place, the work and the people (stock is marked as such), with moderate rounding; images sit beside text in two-column sections and are never decorative. Each image shows what the text names.

## Buttons and links

A clear filled button (rounded rectangle or pill) in the primary color, same style everywhere, plus phone and map links; large enough to tap.

## Motion

Gentle reveals and nothing else; no pinned sections. Reduced motion shows everything at rest.

## Lendable traits

- `hours-card`: A compact card with opening hours, today highlighted, next to the address and the main action.
- `practical-strip`: A strip right after the hero with address, phone and booking in one line each.

## Do

- Put address, hours and the main action within the first two screens.
- Repeat the same action in header, middle and end.
- Use real or honestly labelled sample photos of the place.
- Write short, plain sentences.

## Don't

- No giant outlined words or experimental motion.
- No scene or pinned story sections.
- No hidden contact data behind an interaction.
- No invented testimonials, ratings or claims.
