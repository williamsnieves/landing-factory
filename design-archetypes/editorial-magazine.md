# editorial-magazine

Reads like a magazine feature: multi-column text, drop caps, pull quotes, captions and a strong masthead, with photographs set among the words. A starting grammar, not a template: bend it when the requirement or a reference asks for it, and write the deviation in RUN.md "Design decisions".

## Layout grammar

The page is organized like a long article. A masthead-style opening, then sections built on a column grid: a wide text column with a narrow sidebar for notes, or two or three newspaper-style text columns that flow. A drop cap opens the story, pull quotes break the columns, captions and credits sit under photographs. Sections alternate between text-heavy spreads and a single large photograph with a caption. Rules (thin lines) divide sections and sidebars. Reading comfort comes first.

## Header and footer

Header: a masthead with the name set large and centered or left, a thin rule below, and a small row of section links with a date or issue line. Footer: columns of links, credits and a colophon.

## Typography

A serif for text and headings, with italics for emphasis and captions; a small caps or small sans for section labels, bylines and captions. Body size is generous, line length 60 to 75 characters. Heading scale is moderate to large and varies by section, from a feature title to a small kicker.

## Imagery

Photographs with a caption and a credit line, rectangular, sometimes bleeding off the page edge; a mix of one large image and small supporting ones; black-and-white or toned is fine. Captions are real text.

## Buttons and links

Understated: a text link with an arrow or a small outlined rectangle, in keeping with print. One call to action in a sidebar block or at the end.

## Motion

Very little: a soft reveal on columns, a sticky sidebar at most. The page is for reading. Reduced motion changes nothing important.

## Lendable traits

- `drop-cap`: A large initial letter that opens a text and sinks into the first lines.
- `pull-quote`: A short statement lifted from the text and set large between columns, with a rule.

## Do

- Set long text in columns with a comfortable measure.
- Give every photograph a caption.
- Use rules and small labels to organize the page like print.
- Open with a strong masthead or feature title.

## Don't

- No pill buttons, no tile grids.
- No giant wordmark footer.
- No centered one-column band layout for every section.
- No decoration that does not look printed.
